Journey & Vision

Hey, I'm Amakye-Danso Godson — a full stack developer dedicated to building scalable web ecosystems.

I believe exceptional software lives at the intersection of robust backend logic, resilient database modeling, and uncompromising user-centric aesthetic design. Whether creating high-impact platforms for education like Olistar Edu, automating institutional management with Olistar SMS, or streamlining business finance through SlipSmart, my goal is always the same: make complex processes effortless and reliable.

I am currently pursuing a B.Ed in JHS Education (ICT Major, Maths Minor) at St. Ambrose College of Education (2025 to date), where I also serve on the college IT Support Team — Web Development. My path into engineering includes CS50 Computer Science (Harvard · edX, 2025), IBM Web Development on edX (2026), a Diploma in IT from Kodabs Institute of Information Technology in Techiman (2024), and WASSCE General Science with IT from Akumfi Ameyaw Senior High/Technical School (2023).

Based in Sunyani, Ghana, my toolchain spans modern JavaScript, React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, and cloud infrastructure on Render and Netlify. I treat performance, security, and developer ergonomics as first-class citizens.
